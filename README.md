## Student Information System for SwissLink Higher Institute of Business and Technology Kumba and Bonaberi - Douala

<p>
 This project, Student Management System was carried out to automate the manual processes of student registration and payment of fees at SwissLink University. It was observed that students have to be physically present on campus to do registration and equally payment of fees. Despite the availability of different technological choices which can facilitate these processes, this project was analysed using the Unified Modelling Language (UML). The Rapid Application Development (RAD) was used as the system development approach. The system was developed using HTML, CSS, Vue.js, PHP, MySQL leading to a design and development of a Web Based Student Management System for SHIBAT which does not require the physical presence of students on campus but to remotely register, pay fees and get information about academic status.
</p>
